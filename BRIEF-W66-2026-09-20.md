  did not. Nav is DATA-DRIVEN: `navItems` array in
  `components\Sidebar\Sidebar.tsx`, dispatched by a generic
  `navigate(item.path)`. A grep filtered on `to=|navigate|href|onClick` MISSES
  it. **A FILTERED GREP PRODUCES CONFIDENT FALSE NEGATIVES ON DATA-DRIVEN
  CODE** - route maps, handler registries, nav tables.

  (USB Audio and HID) (0573:1573)`, `SAMSUNG (...)`, `Speakers (USB Audio and
  HID) (0573:1573)`. `Default - X` and `Communications - X` are ALIASES.
